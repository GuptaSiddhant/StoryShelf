StoryShelf can read a build's results and tell you what it thinks: *is this change intended, does it need a human look, or does it look like a regression?* It can also summarize a project's health over the last 30 days. Both are **advisory only** — they never approve, reject, waive a failing `play` function, or override a changed baseline. You stay in charge of every merge decision.

## What you get

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Build triage" icon="approve-check">
    A verdict (`likely-intended`, `needs-review`, `likely-regression`), a short summary, and a note per snapshot — right above the review workspace. [How it works](./triage/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Project health" icon="star">
    A 30-day digest with a verdict, a 0–100 score and trends, on the Library and Builds pages and as a badge on the project card. [How it works](./health/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your models, your keys" icon="puzzle">
    OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Bedrock, Vertex, Azure, a gateway, or a local model. StoryShelf never sees your keys. [Set it up](./setup/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Private by design" icon="magnifier">
    Off until a site admin enables a profile per project. Secrets are redacted, output is plain text, and traces never contain prompts or screenshots. [Privacy and safety](./privacy/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Turn it on

<Steps>

1. **Give the server a model.** Install `@storyshelf/ai` and a provider package, create an AI surface, and pass it to `createShelfApp`. See [Setup and providers](./setup/).

2. **Choose a profile for a project.** A site admin opens **Project settings → AI** (or sets `aiProfile` over the API). Until then the project sends nothing anywhere.

3. **Review as usual.** The AI triage panel appears on the review page, and approvers and admins can generate or regenerate it. Generate the health digest from the project's Library page.

</Steps>

## Cost and control

Everything is bounded: a daily token budget across all instances, a per-project hourly call limit, capped evidence size, and identical evidence is served from cache for free. Site admins get alerts at 50%, 75%, 90% and 100% of the budget and a usage page. See [Budget and usage](./budget/).
