The public demo at **`https://storyshelf.fly.dev`** is a full StoryShelf server (Fly `iad`, SQLite + local storage + Playwright). It shows one project, `storyshelf-ui`: StoryShelf's own Storybook, uploaded by CI on every change. Sign in with the published read-only account below to look around.

## Access

Sign in with the demo account:

- **Email:** `demo@storyshelf.js.org`
- **Password:** `<set by the owner>`

It is a **read-only** account and a viewer of `storyshelf-ui` only. It can open the project's builds, the comparison and the published Storybook, and browse the story library. It cannot approve or reject, comment, change settings, or edit its own profile: those answer 403. Other projects are not visible to it.

The demo runs env-driven local-account auth (`@storyshelf/auth`) with the account marked read-only through `SHELF_READ_ONLY_ACCOUNTS` (see [Auth](/guides/auth/)). With auth off on a local fork (`SHELF_ADMIN_PASSWORD` unset) the UI is open.
