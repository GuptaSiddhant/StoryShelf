## Up and running in three steps

<Steps>

1. **Scaffold a server**

   <PackageManagers type="dlx" pkg="storyshelf" args="server init" />

2. **Upload a Storybook build from CI**

   <PackageManagers type="dlx" pkg="storyshelf" args="upload" />

3. **Review the diffs in your browser** and approve or reject each change, or gate merges on the result.

</Steps>

Full walkthrough: [Getting started](./guides/getting-started/).

## What you get

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="A real review workspace" icon="approve-check">
    Filmstrip of changed stories, side-by-side, swipe, onion-skin and flip comparison, pixel-level zoom, and keyboard-driven approve/reject that jumps to the next change.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Branch-aware baselines" icon="puzzle">
    Baselines per branch with fallback to the default branch. Approve once, auto-approve on `main`, and keep long-lived branches with `persistent` labels.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Server-side capture" icon="rocket">
    Upload your built Storybook; the server renders every story with Playwright (Chromium, Firefox, WebKit) or lightweight Puppeteer. Affected-only capture keeps CI fast.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Published Storybook" icon="open-book">
    Share the latest build at `/storybook` or a per-project subdomain, gated to public branches.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Merge gate and notifications" icon="comment">
    Commit statuses for GitHub and GitLab, threaded comments, and Slack, Teams or email alerts when a build needs review.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI triage and health (optional)" icon="star">
    Bring your own model and get a verdict and per-snapshot notes on every build, plus a 30-day project health digest. Advisory only, off by default, secrets redacted.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Self-host anywhere" icon="cloud">
    Docker Compose locally, or Terraform stacks for AWS, Azure and GCP. Bring your own database, storage and queue.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## AI that helps you review

Optional and advisory. Connect any model through the Vercel AI SDK (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Bedrock, Vertex, Azure, a gateway, or a local model) and StoryShelf will tell you whether a build's changes look intended, need a human look, or look like a regression, and summarize how healthy each project is.

- **Triage on the review page**: a verdict, a short summary and a note per snapshot, generated on demand and cached.
- **Project health**: a score, verdict and trends on the Library page and as a badge on the project card.
- **Under your control**: off until a site admin enables a profile per project, a daily token budget across all instances, and prompts and screenshots never leave your chosen model or your traces.

## Chromatic comparison at a glance

| | Chromatic (SaaS) | StoryShelf (self-hosted) |
|---|---|---|
| **Pricing** | 5k snapshots free, then $179/mo per 35k | MIT, unlimited — infra cost only |
| **Browsers** | Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (cloud) | Chromium/Firefox/WebKit per project + lightweight Puppeteer |
| **Hosting** | Chromatic cloud only | Your infra — Docker, Fly, Vercel, Lambda, Azure, GCP |
| **Data** | US/EU regions | Your DB/storage — full residency |
| **Lock-in** | Proprietary | Open source, standard `/api/v1` |

Full breakdown and migration guide: [Comparison](./guides/chromatic-comparison/) · [Migration](./guides/chromatic-migration/).
